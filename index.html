Kyma

05

Now building. A sound tool for creators with three modes, and a mascot for each

The story

Creators lose hours hunting stock music that almost fits. Kyma makes the sound instead, in under twenty seconds, and gives you three ways to ask for it.

Hum it, wish for it, or set the room. Each mode answers a different moment in an edit, and each has its own character, so the product and the brand tell one story.

Hum & BeatBoxMagicAtmosphere Tap a rhythm on the wave

A glimpse of Hum & BeatBox mode: tap a rhythm for four seconds and it comes back as a loop. The real tool listens to your voice.

Mode 01

Hum & BeatBox

Hum a tune or beatbox a rhythm. Kyma hears the idea and plays it back as a finished track.

MascotCuethe monkey

Quick, rhythmic and a born mimic. Cue repeats what he hears, then makes it better.

Mode 02

Magic

No input needed. A few clicks, and Kyma composes an original soundtrack for the moment.

MascotWooferthe golden retriever

Eager and reliable. Ask once, and Woofer fetches exactly what you wanted.

Mode 03

Atmosphere

Sound for the space around the story: ambience, texture and effects that set the room.

MascotLothe cow

Slow, low and unhurried. Lo is the hum of a place you want to stay in.

Mascot strategy

Every mode has a face. Cue, Woofer and Lo lead Kyma's outreach and its product conversations, so a creator meets a character before a feature. The mascot tells you which mode you are in and what kind of help to expect.

The tool

  1. Pick a mode. Hum & BeatBox, Magic or Atmosphere.
  2. Kyma composes. An original soundtrack or sound effect, made for the moment, not pulled from a shelf.
  3. Under twenty seconds. Sound in hand, with no stock library to dig through.

About

06

A storyteller first. Industrial designer, creative director and creative technologist by method

Eleven years across fintech, mobility and consumer brands, from metal cards taken through factory floors in New Jersey and Philadelphia to products people open every day. I curate the narrative, lead the teams that tell it and build the tools it needs.

A brand is felt as one story, not a stack of deliverables. So the work moves between strategy, image and code: an identity in two scripts, a generative pipeline for a gold card, a browser instrument built because the right one didn't exist. Experign is the studio where those threads meet.
